# Software and data rights

The project authors have not designated a software license for this repository.
Public availability alone does not grant a general permission to reuse or
redistribute the code. A license may be added when the rights holders choose
the terms.

Third-party notices under `third_party/licenses/` apply to the named
dependencies only. Ollama, Qwen model weights, OriginPro, and other dependencies
retain their own terms. OriginPro is a separate licensed application and is
not distributed in this repository.

The baseline perovskite database and literature-derived records are separate
from the software. Publication identifiers, evidence excerpts, and source
hashes retain their research-provenance role; they do not confer rights to the
underlying papers. Full-text source papers, private retrieval locators,
credentials, and the record-level graph containing those locators are excluded
from the manuscript materials.

Software and data licensing remain matters for the rights holders before
depositing a reusable journal archive. No author approval or license grant is
implied by the release validation results.
