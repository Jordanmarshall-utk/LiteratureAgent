# Primary stability eligibility

Record `Ref_ID=28`, DOI `10.1039/c9ta01893j`, composition
`Cs0.05FA0.788MA0.162PbBr0.51I2.49`, contains incompatible stability
descriptions: 75% retention after 5 h and T80 of 200 h. A trace below 80%
after 5 h cannot also have an observed T80 of 200 h under the same sample
and test conditions. Retained publication evidence does not resolve which
description belongs to the tested device.

The primary analysis therefore excludes this record from six targets:
end-retention regression, end retention at least 80%, T80 at least 100 h,
T80 at least 500 h, ln(1 + T80/h), and calculated ln(k). Other targets retain
their existing eligibility. Source values remain recorded for provenance.

The exclusion applies to every affected alternative in Table S10 as well as
the selected Figure 5 models. Figure 5 excludes predicted PCE from all
feature sets. Intentional predicted-PCE alternatives appear only in the
supplementary analysis.
