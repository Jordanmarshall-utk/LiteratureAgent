# Benchmark reproducibility note

The release includes the 720 response-level benchmark rows, the archived predefined phrase-list assignment for each of 90 paper-run-task cells, the three LiteratureAgent benchmark-mode prompt tasks, and the retained LiteratureAgent model manifest. The intended design used one response from each of three task categories in every system–paper–run group. The archived workbook preserves two DeepSeek run-1 prompt-assignment anomalies: Paper 2 repeats the synthesis-materials task and lacks the perovskite-compounds task, while Paper 7 repeats the synthesis-materials task and lacks the synthesis-conditions task. Consequently, 238 of 240 system–paper–run groups contain all three intended categories even though every system still has 90 archived rows. Twenty-nine of the 30 paper-task combinations used a fixed phrase list across all three runs. The Paper 4 synthesis-materials list contained three phrases in run 1 and four in runs 2–3. Uncorrected archived strings and the two assignment anomalies are preserved rather than silently normalized because the aggregate scores were computed against them. The exact historical metric-scoring implementation was not retained. Exact versions, access dates, generation settings, and hidden system instructions for the seven comparator products were also not frozen. These materials support audit and independent rescoring under a newly specified implementation, but they do not guarantee byte-for-byte recreation of the historical metric values or a fully reproducible matched-inference comparison.
